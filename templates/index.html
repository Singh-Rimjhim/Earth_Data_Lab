📖 Scientific Methodology, Citations & Known Limitations

Climatological Baseline: The anomalies are reported in degrees Celsius (°C) with respect to the 1951–1980 baseline period. The estimated absolute global mean surface air temperature for that period is 14.0°C (57.2°F).

Primary Citation: NASA Goddard Institute for Space Studies (GISTEMP Team, 2025: GISS Surface Temperature Analysis, version 4; Lenssen et al., 2019, JGR Atmospheres).

  • Pre-1900 observations rely on sparse weather stations, primarily in Europe and North America; oceanic coverage relies on historical ship bucket measurements.
  • Spatial homogenization and Urban Heat Island (UHI) corrections are performed, but local microclimate uncertainties exist.
  • A statistical trend line demonstrates empirical historical change; attribution to greenhouse gas forcing vs internal climate cycles requires physical climate model simulations (e.g., CMIP6).
